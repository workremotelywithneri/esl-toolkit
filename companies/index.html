Beyond the companies

Where teachers actually find these jobs

Most of these companies don't run big job-board ads — hiring happens inside Facebook groups and pages, almost daily. Join a few, watch how current teachers talk about pay and management, and apply straight from there.

⚠️

Feel free to join and hang out in these groups — but always stay careful. Most members and admins are kind and helpful, but not everyone is. These groups are run by regular people, not checked or verified by this site. Before you give your ID, pay any fee, or share your bank details, check the company or "recruiter" yourself first. A real job will never ask you to pay just to apply. And these aren't the only ones — there are many more groups and pages out there, so try searching Facebook for "ESL teacher Philippines" or similar words to find them.

Small note: I'm not affiliated with or paid by any of the coaches listed here. I personally joined one of their trainings and it was genuinely good — but if you like learning at your own pace, you can totally do it yourself too.
