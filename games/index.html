How to Play Our ESL Games

Simple, interactive classroom rewards designed for teacher-guided and independent play.

1

Choose a Game

Pick a game that fits your lesson or reward activity.

2

Open & Play

Use the game in your browser or display it in your online classroom.

3

Let Students Choose

Students can say numbers, name items, or circle their choices.

4

Make Their Moves

In ClassIn, the teacher clicks, drags, or moves items based on students' choices.

ClassIn Note

When using ClassIn's built-in browser, the teacher controls the game. Students can participate by circling, speaking, or choosing what happens next. The teacher makes the moves for them.

Teacher controls the game. Students make the choices. Everyone gets to play!
