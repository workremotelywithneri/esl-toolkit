    About the Author

    Hi, I'm Neri.

    Former high school teacher. Current ESL teacher. Still learning, creating, and figuring things out along the way.

    Why I created the ESL Toolkit

    It all started with a simple guide.

    When I began my online ESL journey, I spent hours searching for information and advice. I created the guide to make things a little easier for teachers who were just starting out.

    But as time went by, I noticed things I wanted to improve in my own teaching—from classroom activities to student engagement and teaching resources.

    And being someone who loves creating things, I thought, why not make my own?

    That's how a simple guide gradually grew into the ESL Toolkit—a collection of interactive games, practical tools, and resources that I create, use, and continue to improve.

    I'm still learning as I go, and I hope what I create makes your teaching journey a little easier, too.
