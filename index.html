About the Author

Hi, I'm Neri.

Former high school teacher. Current ESL teacher. Still figuring things out.

Why I created this guide

When I was learning about online ESL, the information felt scattered. I spent hours searching Facebook groups, watching YouTube videos, reading comments, and jumping between websites just to figure out where to start.

I made this guide to gather the advice I wished I had as a beginner in one place. It comes from real teaching experience, trial and error, and plenty of learning. I hope it helps other teachers spend less time searching and more time preparing.

If you'd like to read the personal story of how I found my way into ESL, you can find it in the guide.
